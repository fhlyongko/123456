Select one claim requiring verification. Check it against an original or official source. Record the source and result.

Decide whether to accept, modify, reject or defer an AI suggestion, and explain why.

Week 1: record your learning goal and AI-use principles. Week 13: compare technologies that restore, replace or extend human abilities. Record an English sentence before and after revision, with your reason.

5. AI 사용 여부·도구·목적·검증 행동을 적고, 최종 선택을 자신의 말로 설명하세요.
