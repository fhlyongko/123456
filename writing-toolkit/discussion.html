신중한 표현 (Hedging)

단정 대신 한계를 남기는 학술 표현.

강한 단정 → 신중한 표현
"This policy increases growth." → "The findings suggest that this policy may increase growth."
"X causes Y." → "X appears to be associated with Y."
"The program is effective." → "The program appears effective in certain contexts."
자주 쓰는 표현: suggest, indicate, may, appear to, tend to, in certain contexts
