Academic Discussion and Response Board

This section provides academically appropriate response structures for conference discussion. The prompts are designed to support concise, evidence-based, and reflective answers during post-presentation Q&A sessions.

Question on Research Rationale

Explain why the topic matters, how it addresses an educational problem, and how it extends prior scholarship.

Question on Methodology

Briefly clarify participants, data sources, analytic procedures, and the rationale for the selected research design.

Question on Interpretation

Interpret findings within the limits of the evidence and avoid claims that exceed the scope of the data.

Question on Future Research

Connect the limitation to a clear next step, such as larger samples, longitudinal data, comparative analysis, or performance-based measures.

Recommended response structure: “Thank you for this important question. In this study, we interpreted the issue as ... . However, because ... remains a limitation, future research should examine ... in greater depth.”
