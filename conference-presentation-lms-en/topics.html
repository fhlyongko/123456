Presentation Topic Board

The presentation topics are organized by research domain so that viewers can identify the central research questions, intended audiences, and academic contributions before opening the full materials.

AI

AI Literacy and Generative AI

This domain examines AI literacy, generative AI use, prompt practices, ethical awareness, and students' critical engagement with AI-supported learning environments.

GC

Global Competence

This domain explores how students and pre-service teachers develop global competence through flexible major pathways, English education, intercultural learning, and reflective academic experiences.

EF

English Language Teaching and EFL

This domain addresses EFL learning, English teacher education, academic communication, and the pedagogical implications of emerging technologies in language education.

01Research Context

Clarify the educational setting and the problem that motivates the study.

02Research Question

State what the presentation seeks to examine, explain, or evaluate.

03Method

Summarize participants, data sources, analytic procedures, and trustworthiness.

04Findings

Present evidence-based patterns without overstating the scope of the data.

05Implications

Connect findings to curriculum, pedagogy, policy, or future research.

06Discussion

Prepare concise responses for methodology, validity, limitations, and transferability.
