2026 Program

2026 International Field-Based Teacher Education

This section summarizes the 2026 visit sequence, from arrival and school-based fieldwork to the Pusan National University seminar and the concluding AI education presentation.

2026 Academic Inquiry Focus

  • Korean secondary and elementary English language education
  • Teacher professionalism, curriculum, and school culture
  • Participation in a Busan-based academic seminar
  • Recent AI-related developments in Korean education
Day 0

Arrival and Local Orientation

Participants arrive in Changwon, complete check-in, and establish the social and cultural context for the academic visit.

Day 1

Campus Tour and Daecheong High School Visit

The day combines university orientation with a high school visit and professional dialogue with school leaders and teachers.

Day 2

Changwon Jungang High School Teacher Meeting

Participants discuss curriculum, assessment, school culture, and English language education with practicing teachers.

Day 3

Middle School Visit and Elementary Teacher Interviews

The program extends comparison across school levels through classroom observation and teacher interviews.

Day 4

Pusan National University BK Seminar

Participants engage in academic seminar activities and relate field observations to educational research.

Day 5

Recent AI Trends in Korea

The program concludes with a presentation on AI convergence education and its implications for teacher preparation.

2027 Program

2027 International Field-Based Teacher Education

This section presents the 2027 Spring Utah-Korea field visit for pre-service teachers, with emphasis on language learning, school observation, academic seminars, and intercultural inquiry.

2027 Academic Inquiry Focus

  • Korean language learning and campus orientation
  • Elementary and secondary school-based observation
  • Academic presentation exchange at Pusan National University
  • Interdisciplinary seminars and AI digital textbook materials
Day 0

Dormitory Check-In and Campus Orientation

Participants begin with residence check-in and orientation supported by student international coordinators.

Day 1

Korean Language Session and Cultural Orientation

The program introduces Korean language learning, the House of Changwon, and the Office of International Affairs as part of the local orientation sequence.

Day 2

BK Seminar at Pusan National University

Students from Utah and Pusan National University exchange presentations and participate in academic dialogue.

Day 3

Elementary School Observation

Participants observe classroom instruction and discuss teaching practices at Gagopa and Hoewon Elementary Schools.

Day 4

High School Visit and Interdisciplinary Seminar

The day combines a visit to Changwon Jungang High School with seminars on interdisciplinary undergraduate education and international relations.

Day 5

Korean Language Session and Transfer to Seoul

The program concludes with a Korean language session, transfer to Seoul, and independent follow-up activities.

Inquiry Guide

Teacher Interview and Reflective Inquiry Guide

Use these prompt cards to prepare, conduct, and analyze professional conversations with English teachers in Korean school contexts.

School Life and Culture

How do cleaning routines, recess, vacation periods, clubs, sports, and extracurricular activities illuminate everyday school life?

Curriculum and Assessment

How is the curriculum designed, how are standardized tests used, and how do students enter different types of high schools?

Teachers' Professional Roles

How do teachers prepare lessons, support students with different learning needs, and interpret the most challenging and rewarding aspects of teaching?

Families and Private Education

How do homework, private academies, parental expectations, and public or private funding shape students' educational experiences?

Suggested use: Select three questions before each school visit, document teacher responses after the visit, and organize the analysis around educational culture, teacher professionalism, and student support systems.
