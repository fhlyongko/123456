2026 Program

2026 Program

This section summarizes the 2026 visit schedule from February 22 to February 27, with emphasis on school visits, teacher interviews, university seminars, and academic presentations.

Day 0

Arrival in Changwon and Program Orientation

Participants arrive at Changwon Jungang Station, complete hotel check-in, and begin the program through an informal orientation to the local context.

Day 1

Campus Tour and High School Visit

Participants tour Changwon National University, visit Daecheong High School in Gimhae, and meet with staff from the university’s global engagement offices.

Day 2

Professional Dialogue with High School English Teachers

The program includes a structured meeting with English teachers from Changwon Jungang High School to examine Korean secondary English education and school culture.

Day 3

Middle School Visit and Elementary English Teacher Interviews

Participants visit Banlim Middle School and engage in professional dialogue with elementary English teachers to compare school-level practices across educational contexts.

Day 4

BK Seminar at Pusan National University and Busan Field Experience

The program includes participation in a BK seminar at Pusan National University and a field experience in Busan, including the Haeundae area.

Day 5

Presentation on Recent AI Trends in Korea

The program concludes with an academic presentation on AI convergence education in Korea, followed by travel to Seoul and return arrangements.

2027 Program

2027 Program

This section presents the 2027 Spring Utah Korea Visit Program for pre-service teachers, with a focus on Korean language learning, school observation, university seminars, and intercultural academic exchange.

Day 0

Dormitory Check-In and Campus Orientation

Participants check in at the Changwon National University dormitory and complete a campus orientation supported by student global coordinators.

Day 1

Korean Language Session and Cultural Orientation

The day includes a Korean language session, an English-guided visit to the House of Changwon, and a meeting with the Office of International Affairs.

Day 2

BK Seminar at Pusan National University

Participants attend a seminar hosted by the Department of Education at Pusan National University, including presentations by students from Utah and Pusan National University.

Day 3

Elementary School Observation and Teacher Dialogue

The program includes visits to Gagopa Elementary School and Hoewon Elementary School, with classroom observation and discussion sessions with teachers.

Day 4

High School Visit and Interdisciplinary Seminar

Participants visit Changwon Jungang High School and attend seminars connected to global undeclared majors and international relations.

Day 5

Korean Language Session and Transfer to Seoul

The program concludes with a Korean language session, followed by travel to Seoul and independent activities.

Discussion Guide

Teacher Interview and Discussion Guide

This section organizes key questions for professional conversations with English teachers in Korea.

School Life and Culture

Questions address school cleaning culture, recess, vacation practices, clubs, sports, and extracurricular opportunities.

Curriculum and Assessment

Questions examine curriculum design, standardized testing, high school types, and admissions pathways.

Teachers’ Professional Roles

Questions explore teacher qualification, lesson preparation, learner support, and strategies for supporting struggling or gifted students.

Families and Private Education

Questions compare homework, cram schools, parental expectations, compulsory education, and public or private funding structures.

Suggested use: Select three questions before each school visit, document the responses after the visit, and organize the analysis around Korean educational culture, teacher professionalism, and student support systems.
