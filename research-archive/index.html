Research Focus

연구 분야 · Research Interests

AI LiteracyEnglish Language TeachingGenerative AI English-Medium Instruction (EMI)Teacher Education Multilingual EducationGlobal Competence

영어교육·교사교육·AI/디지털교육과 EMI·다중언어 교육 맥락을 연결해, 영어교육 이론과 실제 수업 설계, AI 도구 활용, 학습자 다양성을 통합하는 연구를 수행합니다.

My research examines how AI literacy, generative AI, and digital learning can support English language teaching and teacher education. I also investigate English-medium instruction, multilingual education, and global competence, with attention to learner diversity and classroom practice.
