One research trajectory, three stages.

The collection moves beyond a simple “benefits versus risks” debate. Across three years, the central question shifts from what students think about AI to how purposeful use should be designed and taught.

2024

Perception and classroom integration

Initial studies examine university students’ perceptions of AI-based writing tools and what changes when generative AI enters general English courses.

2025

Strategy and cognitive-affective experience

The program then investigates how students use AI across stages of EFL writing and how motivation, cognitive load, and satisfaction interact.

Risk awareness, readiness, and strategic integration

Later studies focus on balanced use, educational support, and the capacities learners need to evaluate and direct AI-generated language.
