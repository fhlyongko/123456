Purpose

A Research Archive for Scholarly Reading and Inquiry

The archive is designed for students, researchers, and instructors who need a structured overview of empirical studies on generative AI in English language learning. It supports both content learning and academic reading practice.

Content Learning

Understanding AI-Mediated English Learning

The collection helps readers examine what generative AI can support in English learning and where risks such as overreliance, reduced independent thinking, and academic misconduct may emerge.

Research Literacy

Understanding Empirical Article Structure

Each paper is organized by bibliographic information, research focus, method, findings, implications, and discussion questions so that readers can trace the logic from research question to conclusion.

Academic Use

From Reading to Research Design

The site highlights how a coherent research program develops from perception studies to classroom implementation, learner strategy analysis, cognitive and affective outcomes, and readiness for AI-supported learning.

Research Map

How the Seven Studies Connect

The collection shows a cumulative research trajectory. It begins with how students perceive AI-based writing tools and expands toward pedagogical integration, strategic use, learning outcomes, and the need for formal AI literacy instruction.

2024

Perception and Classroom Integration

Initial studies examine university students' perceptions of AI-based writing tools and explore what changes when generative AI is incorporated into general English courses.

2025

Strategy and Cognitive-Affective Experience

The research program then investigates how students use AI across stages of EFL writing and how motivation, cognitive load, and satisfaction interact in AI-integrated learning.

2026

Strategic Integration, Risk Awareness, and Readiness

Later studies examine AI-integrated writing strategies, cognitive and affective advantages and risks, and students' preparation for effective AI use in English learning.
