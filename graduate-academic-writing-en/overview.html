Overview

Course Overview

This course supports graduate students in developing a complete academic writing process: designing a researchable problem, building a literature-based argument, writing paper sections, revising with reviewer expectations in mind, and preparing ethically responsible research manuscripts.

Planning

Research Design

Students narrow broad interests into researchable problems and formulate research questions that can be answered through appropriate evidence and methods.

Writing

Paper Composition

The course explains the communicative functions of abstracts, introductions, methods, results, discussions, and conclusions in English-medium academic writing.

Ethics

Research Integrity

Students examine informed consent, IRB procedures, responsible citation, plagiarism prevention, data protection, and transparent AI-assisted writing practices.

Publication

Scholarly Development

The course connects manuscript preparation to peer review, revision, submission planning, academic portfolios, and long-term researcher identity development.

Learning Design

From Research Idea to Manuscript Readiness

The course uses a recursive cycle of preparation, in-class writing, peer or instructor feedback, revision, and portfolio consolidation. By the end of the course, students should be able to explain how each section of a research paper contributes to a coherent scholarly argument.

01

Define

Identify a research problem, situate it in a field, and specify the gap that justifies the study.

02

Align

Connect the purpose, questions, data, methods, analysis, and contribution into one logical design.

03

Draft

Produce section-level academic writing with clear claims, cautious language, and evidence-based reasoning.

04

Revise

Use feedback and reviewer criteria to improve clarity, coherence, ethical transparency, and contribution.
