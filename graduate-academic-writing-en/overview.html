Course overview / 01

Research becomes publishable when its parts form one argument.

This course supports graduate students in formulating a researchable problem, constructing a literature-based argument, drafting manuscript sections, revising in response to reviewer expectations, and preparing ethically responsible research manuscripts.

Four foundations

What the course develops.

Each foundation is treated as part of the same scholarly system. Strong language cannot compensate for weak alignment, and sound design cannot compensate for unclear reporting.

01

Planning

Research design

Refine broad academic interests into researchable problems and formulate questions that can be addressed through appropriate evidence, methods, and analytic reasoning.

02

Writing

Manuscript development

Examine the communicative functions of abstracts, introductions, methods, results, discussions, and conclusions in English-language research articles.

03

Ethics

Research integrity

Address informed consent, IRB procedures, responsible citation, plagiarism prevention, data protection, and transparent AI-assisted writing practices.

04

Publication

Scholarly development

Connect manuscript preparation with peer review, revision, submission planning, academic portfolios, and long-term researcher identity development.

Learning design

A recursive cycle, not a linear checklist.

The course moves repeatedly through preparation, guided drafting, peer or instructor feedback, revision, and portfolio consolidation. Students learn to explain how every section contributes to a coherent scholarly argument.

  1. Define

    Identify a research problem, situate it in a field, and specify the gap that justifies the study.

  2. Align

    Connect the purpose, questions, data, methods, analysis, and contribution into a coherent design.

  3. Draft

    Produce section-level academic writing with clear claims, cautious language, and evidence-based reasoning.

  4. Revise

    Use feedback and reviewer criteria to strengthen clarity, coherence, ethical transparency, and contribution.
