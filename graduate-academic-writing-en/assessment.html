Course Operation and Assessment

The course emphasizes iterative writing: preparation before class, guided drafting during class, revision after feedback, and cumulative portfolio development across the semester.

01

Research Question Brief

A one-page brief that defines the topic, research gap, purpose, and research questions.

02

Literature Review Matrix

A comparative matrix that organizes previous studies and identifies a defensible research gap.

03

Paper Section Draft

A developed draft of one major manuscript section, such as the abstract, introduction, methods, or results.

04

Final Research Portfolio

A curated portfolio that includes drafts, revision notes, ethical reflection, and presentation materials.

Research Alignment

The purpose, questions, methods, data, analysis, interpretation, and contribution should form a coherent scholarly argument.

Academic Expression

The writing should demonstrate clarity, accurate terminology, paragraph-level coherence, appropriate citation, and cautious academic tone.

Revision Process

Students should respond to feedback, explain revision decisions, and show measurable improvement across draft versions.

Ethics and AI Transparency

Students should demonstrate responsible research conduct, careful data handling, plagiarism prevention, and transparent use of AI tools.
