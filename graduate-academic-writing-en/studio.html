Writing studio / 03

Use a frame to begin. Revise until it becomes yours.

These sentence frames support classroom practice and independent drafting. Adapt every template to your discipline, then revise it for conceptual accuracy, disciplinary fit, and an appropriate scholarly tone.

How to use the studio

Copy a frame, replace the placeholders, verify the underlying claim, and revise the language so that it accurately reflects your study. A sentence frame is a drafting aid—not evidence and not a substitute for scholarly judgment.
