03 Profile

Yong-Jik Lee · Educator & researcher

Learning design begins with a question: What enables learners to act?

Yong-Jik Lee’s research and teaching connect English language education, educational technology, and learner agency. His current work examines AI literacy and the pedagogical use of generative AI in university language education.

This archive documents an earlier stage of that trajectory: a sustained engagement with multilingual education, active learning, critical pedagogy, and technology-supported instruction.
