Framework

Core AI Literacy Dimensions

This center frames AI literacy as a practical academic competence: understanding AI systems, evaluating output, using tools ethically, and designing learning activities that preserve student agency.

  • Conceptual UnderstandingStudents learn what AI systems can and cannot do in educational and academic contexts.
  • Critical EvaluationStudents examine accuracy, bias, source quality, evidence, and the limitations of generated output.
  • Responsible UseStudents practice transparent, ethical, and accountable forms of AI-assisted learning.
  • Learning DesignInstructors connect AI tools with feedback, writing, discussion, and reflective learning tasks.
